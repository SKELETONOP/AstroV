Family approval is often the hardest part of an inter-caste marriage — not the relationship itself. This service focuses on easing that resistance calmly, rather than forcing a confrontation.

<blockquote class="pull-quote">
  "Approval rarely arrives through argument. It arrives when fear is replaced with reassurance."
</blockquote>

## Where families usually resist

<div class="callout">
  <p class="font-semibold text-neutral-900">Common concerns raised by families</p>
  <ul class="mt-2 list-disc pl-5 text-sm text-neutral-700 space-y-1">
    <li>Social perception and extended family opinion</li>
    <li>Differences in customs or religious practice</li>
    <li>Concerns about long-term compatibility</li>
    <li>Fear of losing closeness with their child</li>
  </ul>
</div>

## How this is approached

  <div class="step-item">

    <div>

    </div>
  </div>

## The goal

The aim is a marriage that begins with the family's blessing wherever that is achievable, approached with patience rather than pressure.
