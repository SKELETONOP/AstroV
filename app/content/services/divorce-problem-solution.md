Not every marriage heading toward divorce needs to end there. Many disputes escalate under pressure, misunderstanding, or outside interference rather than a genuine end of compatibility.

<blockquote class="pull-quote">
  "Before deciding a marriage is over, it is worth checking whether the marriage is actually the problem."
</blockquote>

## Situations this covers

<div class="callout">
  <p class="font-semibold text-neutral-900">Common triggers</p>
  <ul class="mt-2 list-disc pl-5 text-sm text-neutral-700 space-y-1">
    <li>Frequent arguments over the same recurring issue</li>
    <li>In-law or extended family interference</li>
    <li>Financial stress affecting the relationship</li>
    <li>A partner considering separation impulsively</li>
  </ul>
</div>

## An honest caveat

Where a relationship genuinely cannot continue safely or fairly, that will be said plainly. Remedies aim to restore a healthy marriage — not to prolong a harmful one.
