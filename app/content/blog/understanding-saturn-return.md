Roughly once every 27 to 29 years, Saturn returns to the exact position it held in your birth chart. Most people notice it without knowing what it is: a period that feels heavier, slower, and more demanding of discipline than usual.

<blockquote class="pull-quote">
  "Saturn does not punish. It audits — and asks you to build on ground that will actually hold."
</blockquote>

## Why it feels difficult

Saturn is the planet of structure, responsibility and long timelines. Its return tends to surface exactly the areas of life that were built on shortcuts: a career chosen for convenience, a relationship left unresolved, habits that were never quite sustainable.

<div class="callout">
  <p class="font-semibold text-neutral-900">Signs you may be in a Saturn return</p>
  <ul class="mt-2 list-disc pl-5 text-sm text-neutral-700 space-y-1">
    <li>A sudden urge to restructure your career or finances</li>
    <li>Relationships that either deepen commitment or end</li>
    <li>Increased responsibility toward family or health</li>
    <li>A general sense that "easy" options have stopped working</li>
  </ul>
</div>

Most Saturn returns last two to three years within the tightest orb. What is built during that window — a habit, a decision, a boundary — tends to hold for a long time afterward. If you are unsure whether you're in this period, a full chart review can confirm it and show exactly what it is asking of you.
