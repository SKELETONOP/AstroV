Bad luck happens to everyone. But when several unrelated problems appear together, in the same household, over a short period, it is worth checking whether something more specific is going on.

<blockquote class="pull-quote">
  "One bad month is circumstance. Four unrelated crises in the same house, in the same season, is a pattern worth checking."
</blockquote>

## Five signs worth noticing

<div class="callout">
  <p class="font-semibold text-neutral-900">Common patterns</p>
  <ol class="mt-2 list-decimal pl-5 text-sm text-neutral-700 space-y-1">
    <li>Frequent, unexplained arguments among people who normally get along</li>
    <li>Plants or pets in the home becoming unwell without clear cause</li>
    <li>Persistent fatigue or disturbed sleep affecting multiple household members</li>
    <li>Repeated appliance or structural issues in a short window</li>
    <li>A sense of heaviness in a specific room or corner of the house</li>
  </ol>
</div>

## What to do about it

<div class="space-y-5 my-8">
  <div class="step-item">
    <span class="step-number">1</span>
    <div>
      <h3 class="text-base font-semibold text-neutral-900 mt-0 mb-1">Rule out the obvious first</h3>
      <p class="text-sm text-neutral-600 m-0">Check practical causes — damp, poor ventilation, financial stress — before assuming anything else.</p>
    </div>
  </div>
  <div class="step-item">
    <span class="step-number">2</span>
    <div>
      <h3 class="text-base font-semibold text-neutral-900 mt-0 mb-1">Get a proper reading</h3>
      <p class="text-sm text-neutral-600 m-0">A consultation can confirm whether the pattern points to a genuine energetic cause.</p>
    </div>
  </div>
  <div class="step-item">
    <span class="step-number">3</span>
    <div>
      <h3 class="text-base font-semibold text-neutral-900 mt-0 mb-1">Apply a targeted remedy</h3>
      <p class="text-sm text-neutral-600 m-0">Remedies are most effective when matched to a specific, confirmed cause rather than applied generally.</p>
    </div>
  </div>
</div>

If more than two or three of these signs are present at once, it is reasonable to have the situation reviewed properly rather than waiting for things to settle on their own.
